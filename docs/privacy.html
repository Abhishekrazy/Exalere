Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

1. Privacy-First Commitment

Exalere is committed to protecting your personal privacy. Exalere is built on a local-first architecture. We do not require accounts, logins, emails, credit cards, or passwords to use the software.

2. No Server Telemetry or Tracking

Exalere contains zero third-party tracking analytics, telemetry SDKs, or behavioral profiling:

  • We do not log your IP address.
  • We do not record what media you search for or watch.
  • We do not track device identifiers or location data.
  • We do not sell, share, or monetize any user data.

3. Local Device Data Storage

All user preferences (such as selected dark theme, TV mode toggle, playback progress timestamps, watch history, and favorite titles) are saved exclusively on your local device via secure device storage (such as Android SharedPreferences and Windows local app data files).

Uninstalling Exalere or clearing application data completely and permanently wipes all stored local settings.

4. Direct Third-Party Network Requests

When using Exalere to view posters or stream media, your device establishes direct HTTP/HTTPS connections with third-party service providers (such as the TMDB API for artwork or remote IPTV/stream hosts).

These third-party servers may log standard network requests (including your IP address and user-agent string) in accordance with their own respective privacy policies. You may choose to route your traffic through a trusted VPN or proxy if you wish to obscure your IP from external content hosts.

5. Contact & Questions

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to inspect the transparent source code, you can audit the entire codebase publicly at our GitHub repository.
